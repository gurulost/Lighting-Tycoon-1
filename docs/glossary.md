# Lighting Tycoon Glossary

Use this as a quick reference for every icon, badge, and system.

## Parts (Open Standard)
- Clip (Open): starter part for open builds.
- Track (Open): tier 2 run.
- Segment (Open): tier 3 segment.
- Smart Kit (Open): tier 4 smart kit.
- Premium System (Open): top-tier open system.

## Parts (Locked Certified)
- Clip (Locked): certified part, raises Dependency.
- Track (Locked): tier 2 locked track.
- Segment (Locked): tier 3 locked segment.
- Smart Kit (Locked): tier 4 locked smart kit.
- Premium System (Locked): top-tier locked system.

## Stations
- Workbench: tap to spawn parts; cooldown improves with upgrades.
- Order Inbox: view and fulfill orders.
- R&D Bench: spend research to unlock Freedom tech.

## Utilities
- Backpack: temporary storage; drag items in and out.
- Recycle Bin: delete parts for a small cash/research refund.

## Tactical Boosts
- Supplier Scout: spend cash to skew the next spawns toward Open, Locked, or higher tiers.
- Mentor Workshop Clinic: spend cash to boost the next merges with extra research and lower dependency.
- Baron Warranty Stamp: spend cash to soften wrong-family penalties or boost Baron contract payouts.

## Currencies
- Cash: buy upgrades and expansions.
- Reputation: unlocks neighborhoods and higher-value orders.
- Research: unlocks R&D and the Freedom Controller.

## Dependency + Villain
- Dependency Meter: rises with locked parts; high levels add certified/locked orders.
- Bulb Baron: locked supplier with tempting offers.
- Freedom Controller: converts locked kits to open-compatible parts.

## Order Badges
- Certified: locked preferred or required for full rewards.
- Rush: bonus decays over time (no hard fail).
- Style Match: all Open or all Locked.
- Preference: prefers Open/Locked, wrong family reduces payout.
- Exact Tiers: no substitutions allowed.
- Eco Audit: open kits grant bonus research.
- Lockout: firmware lock; use locked or compatible kits.
